# Mohar Live-Link User Feedback Log

## 8 Interview Questions for Real Content Creators / Reviewers
1. **First Impression (Clarity):** Within the first 15 seconds of opening the link without a login or walkthrough, was it clear what Mohar does and where you should click first?
2. **Source Transparency:** When Mohar flagged an issue in your post, did seeing the exact Rule ID or Fact ID (e.g., `CL-001` or `F-004`) make you trust the flag more than a generic AI rewrite? Why or why not?
3. **Quality of Suggested Fixes:** Did the two suggested fixes (`fix_1` and `fix_2`) preserve your original tone and meaning while fixing the compliance issue, or did you prefer editing the text yourself?
4. **Severity vs. Confidence:** Was the distinction between **Severity** (how serious the rule violation is) and **Confidence** (how sure the system is) easy to understand on the finding cards?
5. **Handling False Alarms:** If Mohar flags something you disagree with, how intuitive was the **Reject with reason** flow for explaining your decision to a manager?
6. **Manager / Reviewer Perspective:** Switching to the **Manager** view, did the version history, creator decision reasons, and audit log give you enough context to approve or request changes confidently?
7. **Friction & Speed:** Did any step feel slow, confusing, or clunky (such as server wake-up time, finding cards, or resubmitting a second version)?
8. **Real-World Adoption:** What is the single biggest improvement Mohar would need before you would use it in your real day-to-day content workflow?
